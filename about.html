Our Story

A foundation for success.

Elite Property is an established student accommodation provider based in Mthatha, Eastern Cape, with a commitment to creating environments where students can live securely, comfortably and with confidence.

Operating since 2015, the business has developed its experience around the needs of students and the standards required to provide dependable accommodation within the tertiary education environment.

Our current property, Copperleaf Residence 2, provides 14-bed NSFAS-accredited student accommodation and primarily serves students attending Walter Sisulu University and surrounding tertiary institutions.

From secure living spaces and furnished bedrooms to connectivity, communal facilities and renewable-energy infrastructure, our approach is centred on creating a practical environment that supports student life.
